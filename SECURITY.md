## 🚨 Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability or accidental credential exposure in Job-Flow Automator, please report it privately:

* **Email:** `georgii.liluashvili@gmail.com`
* **Subject:** `[SECURITY] Job-Flow Automator Vulnerability Report`

Please include:
1. Description of the issue and potential impact.
2. Steps to reproduce or proof-of-concept.
3. Your proposed remediation (if available).

We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and coordinate a fix promptly.
